# Theme assets

Original references: four screenshots supplied by the repository owner, stored in `web/assets/{neon,glass,family,gold}.png`. They are used for theme thumbnails and comparison mode, not as an interactive screenshot overlay.

Production hero backgrounds were generated with the built-in Imagegen tool and saved to `web/assets/hero-{neon,glass,family,gold}.png`. All prompts requested a wide cinematic 16:9 composition, a dark empty left half for interface copy, and no text, letters, UI, icons, logos or watermarks.

Final prompt subjects:

- Neon: Photorealistic modern dark blue living room at night, television on the right showing a luminous blue planet above a mountain lake, teal/amber ambient light, sofa foreground.
- Glass: Photorealistic family of three from behind on the right, watching aurora over a snowy mountain lake, cool teal modern living room.
- Family: Friendly polished 3D animated Indian family of four on the right, father, mother, boy and girl sharing popcorn on a sofa during movie night in a cozy blue room; original characters.
- Gold: Photorealistic luxury black/gold room, family of four from behind on the right, television showing golden sunset over an alpine lake, amber lighting.

Content collection cards use generic original titles. Third-party streaming apps are launched if installed; content is not embedded, licensed or provided by this project.
